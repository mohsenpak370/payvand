A wallet is not tied to a single version of Solana. It keeps a switch with at least two settings:
**mainnet**, where the money is real, and **devnet**, where the same software runs on tokens that are
free and worth nothing. Everything else — the address, the app, the buttons — looks the same.

If you are new to crypto, start on devnet. A payment on mainnet cannot be undone, and the only way
to learn what a button does without that risk is to press it on a network where a mistake costs
nothing but a few seconds.

## Before anything else

You need a wallet installed. [Solflare](https://www.solflare.com/download/) has a browser extension
and a mobile app; Phantom and Backpack work the same way. If you have none yet, the
[getting-started page](/start) walks through installing one and claiming free test tokens.

## Solflare

**Extension:** open the wallet, choose **Settings**, then **Network**, and pick **Devnet**.

**Mobile app:** tap the wallet's name at the top of the screen, choose **Network**, and pick
**Devnet**. Solflare remembers the network per profile, so a separate profile can stay on mainnet
while you practise on devnet.

## Phantom

Open **Settings**, then **Developer settings**, then **Change network**, and choose **Devnet**. If
you do not see Developer settings, update the app first — the menu was added in a later version.

## Backpack

Open **Settings**, choose **Network**, and pick **Devnet**.

## How to be sure

Three signals, and they should agree:

- The wallet itself shows **Devnet** somewhere near the account name or in its network menu.
- The Payvand header shows the small **devnet** badge next to the name.
- Tokens you receive from a faucet appear, and are worth nothing — that is the point.

If any of the three says mainnet, stop and switch before approving anything.

## Getting back to real money

The same menu switches back to **Mainnet**. Your devnet balance stays on devnet: it is not converted,
not transferred, and cannot be sold. Anything that offers to turn test tokens into real money is a
scam.
