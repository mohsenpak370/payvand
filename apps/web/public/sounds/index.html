Payment sound alternatives

Ten short candidates for the two sounds a payment makes. Tap to play — one at a time. The first in each list is what ships today.

All ten are 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, synthesised locally — no samples, nothing to license. Peak-normalised, no clipping. The app currently synthesises its two tones at runtime in app/composables/useTransactionSounds.ts; a chosen file can replace that, or its recipe can be copied into the oscillator code.
